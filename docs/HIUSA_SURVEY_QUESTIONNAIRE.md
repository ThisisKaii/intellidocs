# APPENDIX C — SURVEY QUESTIONNAIRE

**HIUSA: AN AI-INTEGRATED STUDENT GOVERNANCE AND FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM**

Dear Participants,

We respectfully invite you to participate in this survey for HIUSA: An AI-Integrated Student Governance and Financial Management System. The study aims to gather insights on current Student Body Organization (SBO) processes and evaluate the proposed system's functionality, usability, reliability, and overall acceptability.

You are invited because you are a student, current or former SBO officer, or current or former SBO adviser whose experiences and perspectives are relevant to the study. If you agree to participate, you will answer questions about current SBO practices, challenges, proposed system features, and HIUSA. The survey will take approximately 10 to 12 minutes.

There are no anticipated significant risks, although some questions may cause minor discomfort when reflecting on organizational experiences. You may skip any question you do not wish to answer. While there may be no direct personal benefit, your responses may help improve student governance processes and support HIUSA's development.

**Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may refuse to participate or withdraw at any time without penalty.**

**Your Participation Matters**

- **Confidentiality and Anonymity:** Your responses will remain confidential, and no identifying information will be included in the study.
- **Data Security:** Only the research team will have access to the collected data.
- **Academic Purpose:** All information gathered will be used strictly for academic research and presented only in summarized or aggregated form.

Your responses will contribute to the development of a more efficient, transparent, accountable, and AI-assisted student governance and financial management system for Student Body Organizations.

Thank you for your time, cooperation, and support!

Warm regards,
The Research Team
Borgueta, John Carlo L. · Cavan, Ayn Lorebelle E. · Ompad, Mary Jasmin P. · Ventic, Jade Mykel R.
