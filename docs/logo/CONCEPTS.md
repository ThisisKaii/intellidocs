## 10 One-Sentence Concepts

1. **Wordmark**: "IntelliDocs" set in a custom geometric sans where the `t`-crossbars form a subtle document grid, and the `I` and `D` share a vertical stroke as a monogram ligature.

2. **Letterform (I)**: A single `I` constructed as a folded document corner — the vertical stem is the page edge, the top serif folds over at the golden ratio — with a neural spark at the fold point.

3. **Lettermark (ID)**: Interlocked `I` and `D` where the `D`'s counter is a perfect circle (the document hole-punch), and the `I` pierces through as a pen stroke.

4. **Pictorial — Document**: A minimal document sheet (3:4 ratio) with three horizontal lines for text; the top line is a heading (bold), the middle two are body; a subtle fountain pen nib rests at the lower-right corner.

5. **Pictorial — Quill**: A fountain pen nib drawn from two arcs meeting at a point; the slit in the nib is a document icon; the breather hole is a neural node.

6. **Abstract — Convergent Brackets**: Left and right square brackets `[ ]` converging toward center; their inner edges form a neural cluster of three circles at the golden angle (137.5°), symbolising intelligence gathering around the document.

7. **Negative Space — Doc + Brain**: A document silhouette (rectangle with folded corner) where the negative space between the fold and the page forms a brain/profile silhouette — figure/ground reversal.

8. **Negative Space — I + Document**: The letter `I` as a solid vertical bar; its negative space on the right forms a document sheet with folded corner; the `I` dot is a neural spark.

9. **Combination — Quill + Wordmark**: The fountain pen nib (Concept 5) as symbol, left of a custom "IntelliDocs" wordmark where the `t`-crossbars echo the nib's slit; shared stroke weight and corner radius.

10. **Emblem — Academic Seal**: A rounded shield containing a document with folded corner; the document's text lines are the shield's divisions; "IntelliDocs" curves along the top arc, "Est. 2026" along bottom.

## Selection for Development (3 concepts)

Per process: choose 3 distinct types for first presentation.

| Concept | Type | Rationale |
|---------|------|-----------|
| **2. Letterform (I)** | Letterform | Compact for app icon/favicon; "I" = IntelliDocs + Intelligence + Identity; folded corner = academic document |
| **4. Pictorial Document** | Pictorial | Directly represents the product; clear at small sizes; geometric reduction to primitives |
| **6. Abstract Convergent Brackets** | Abstract | Conceptual positioning (intelligence gathering around document); distinctive in academic/tech space; works as app icon |

These three cover: Letterform (compact/tech), Pictorial (product clarity), Abstract (conceptual/ownable). Per decision guide: 5 (app icon first) → Letterform; 3 (vivid image) → Pictorial; 4 (phenomenon) → Abstract.
