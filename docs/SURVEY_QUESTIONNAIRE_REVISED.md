# APPENDIX C — SURVEY QUESTIONNAIRE

**INTELLIDOCS: AN INTELLIGENT DOCUMENT EDITING SYSTEM WITH BEHAVIORAL LEARNING AND AUTOMATED FORMATTING**

We are conducting this survey as part of our capstone project titled "IntelliDocs: An Intelligent Document Editing System with Behavioral Learning and Automated Formatting." This study seeks to understand the challenges that students, faculty, and professionals face with repetitive, mechanical formatting tasks, and how intelligent automation can help reduce cognitive load.

After using the IntelliDocs system, you will be asked to answer a series of questions about your current formatting practices, your acceptance of the system's core modules, and your experience while using it.

**Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may skip any question or stop at any time without penalty. All responses will be treated with strict confidentiality, no personally identifiable information will be collected, and data will be used solely for academic purposes. Participants must be 18 years old and above.**

Your Participation Matters

- **Confidentiality and Anonymity:** Your responses will remain confidential, and no identifying information will be included in the study.
- **Data Security:** Only the research team will have access to the collected data.
- **Academic Purpose:** All information gathered will be used strictly for academic research and presented only in summarized or aggregated form.

Your responses will contribute to the development of a more efficient, intelligent, and user-adaptive document editing system.

Thank you for your time, cooperation, and support!

Warm regards,
The Research Team
