Figure 17: Use case diagram of IntelliDocs — the Student and the Professor are the two primary users. Both can register, create and edit documents, issue natural language commands, receive and respond to formatting suggestions, and utilize the auto-save feature. The Professor additionally reviews student submissions (Figure 25).

System Use Case Model — Professor

Detailed specification taken verbatim from the IntelliDocs manuscript (Figure 25).

Review Document

Figure 25
Use Case Name
Review Document
Purpose
It enables a professor to evaluate a student's submitted document and provide feedback.
Triggering Actor
Professor
Brief Description
The professor views a submitted document, adds comments, and assigns a grade. Adding comments always includes highlighting the relevant text, and grading a document is extended by notifying the student once a grade has been recorded.
Precondition
The professor must be logged in and the student must have submitted the document.
Post-condition
The document is annotated with comments and a grade, and the student is notified.
Steps
USERSYSTEM
1. The professor opens a student's submission.1.1. Loads the document for review.
2. The professor selects text and adds a comment.2.1. Includes Highlight Text and attaches the comment to the selection.
3. The professor assigns a grade and submits it.3.1. Extends Grade Document by notifying the student of the recorded grade.
